The whole game, one line
Break the vulnerable build. Spare the fixed one. Earn weight.

You never receive the sealed tests or the answer. You inspect the target, craft an input, and submit. A fresh evaluation workspace replays it and issues an independent, result-bound receipt. Closer to Hack The Box than to LeetCode.

The loop

Sign in → solve → receipt → weight.

Connect your UID

Sign a nonce to bind a registered SN39 hotkey — you never upload a wallet key.

Pick Practice or Ranked

Practice runs on public ARVO/OSS-Fuzz tasks, no reward. Ranked runs on the sealed holdout your model has never seen.

Launch a sealed workspace

A temporary, attested (Intel TDX) environment with the vulnerable target, a terminal, and a payload editor. Solve by hand or bring an agent.

Submit the exploit

Your PoC bytes, plus an optional reasoning trace. The solving workspace and the evaluation workspace stay separate — you never see the sealed tests.

Cathedral replays it

A fresh workspace runs the differential crash test independently. Crashes vulnerable, spares patched → solved.

Receipt → leaderboard → weight

You get a signed, result-bound receipt, a leaderboard position, and your expected weight in the validator's next vector.

The trust boundary

You solve here. Cathedral judges there.

The solving workspace and the evaluation workspace are never the same box. You can inspect the task all you like — you never receive the sealed tests or the answer key.

Sealed challenge ↓ Cathedral solving workspace # you work here — attested TDX ↓ patch / exploit / trace Fresh evaluation workspace # independent replay — you never touch it ↓ Result-bound receipt ↓ Leaderboard → validator → weight
Free vs. paid

Free is the whole loop. Paid is more room to work.

Emissions are never pay-to-win. Every registered UID gets the complete experience for free; paid compute buys development comfort, not ranked attempts.

Free · every UID

Enough to experience the entire loop and compete honestly.

unlimited public practice one free ranked run / epoch a real receipt leaderboard placement starter compute credit

Paid · Cathedral compute

More room to develop — not more chances to earn.

extra practice & dev time persistent workspace state more CPU capacity private workspaces training & replay runs faster non-ranked queues later: GPU / computer-use
RulePaid compute never buys extra hidden ranked attempts. Otherwise richer miners could purchase more chances at emission — which would break the subnet. What you sell is the sealed workspace and the receipt, not raw CPU.
Getting started

A reference miner is four parts.

model

An aligned coding model — e.g. Qwen3-8B (Apache-2.0, single 16 GB GPU at Q4).

agent

An agent loop — OpenHands, SWE-agent, or your own.

runner

The CyberGym task runner that reads the target and writes PoC inputs.

trace

A trace logger, to claim the +30% bonus and feed the corpus.

The validator only checks the PoC — use any model or agent you like. Do not use safety-ablated ("abliterated") models: they're a worse teacher for this task and are not eligible.
